Application overview

Study planning with a human decision at the center.

StudyMate turns one learning goal into practical calendar sessions. AI recommends; the student reviews, moves, and accepts. That separation keeps the system useful without letting automation make the final commitment.

The four-step experience

1

Describe the goal

Enter subject, total hours, difficulty, preferred time of day, and deadline.

2

Get a recommendation

StudyMate searches available 30-minute openings and caps each session at two hours.

3

Adjust the schedule

Use the monthly overview, then drag the temporary weekly-calendar preview in 30-minute steps.

4

Accept the plan

The server validates the whole plan again, then saves every session atomically.

What the application protects

Realistic time

Morning, afternoon, and evening preferences map to defined study windows. Saved tasks are treated as occupied time, and recommendations stop at the deadline.

Student control

Preview creation never writes a goal or task. Only the explicit Accept Plan action makes the schedule permanent.

Reliable changes

Moves are rejected when they overlap another task, enter the past, exceed the deadline, or alter the original duration. The calendar returns to its previous position and explains why.

Graceful AI failure

Ollama supplies personalized explanations and chat. If it fails or takes more than 12 seconds, StudyMate shows a clearly labelled, deterministic answer based on current schedule data.

How the pieces connect

  1. Browser UISafe form, weekly calendar, preview controls, tasks, and chat.
  2. FastAPIValidation, preview lifecycle, acceptance, status, and scheduling endpoints.
  3. Scheduler + AIFirst-fit calendar logic plus local Ollama explanations with fallback.
  4. JSON storageAtomic persistence to a local data file with stable incremental IDs.

A submission-ready demo

Core story

  1. Create a goal due next week.
  2. Show the recommended sessions.
  3. Drag one by 30 minutes.
  4. Accept and reload.
  5. Move a saved session.

Reliability story

  1. Attempt an overlapping move.
  2. Show the automatic revert and reason.
  3. Stop Ollama.
  4. Create another preview.
  5. Show the visible Fallback label.
Deliberate scope: this version does not import external calendars, convert time zones, or perform advanced optimization. It focuses on a complete, testable recommendation–adjustment–acceptance loop.
